# Surface: the game (index.html)

Scope: whole app: title screen, in-game HUD, pager, radio, shout banners, pause/manual, touch controls, and the canvas world art. Mode: Experience (the game leads).
Audience/job: public browser players, desktop and phone equally; seconds to play, readable mid-chase.
Constraints: touch controls fully playable; HUD must not cover the play area; no gimmicky retro FX (no scanlines, glow, emoji); parody names only (PRODUCT.md).
Wink lands on: shout banners, pager and payphones, car radio, title screen.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The game is the big box it came in. Title screen is the box front, the city is the foldout street map come alive, pause is the manual, HUD pieces are stickers and the manual's key. Refuses dark glass panels over a neon arcade.

OWN-WORLD: Box black with silver-embossed extruded italic display type (Archivo Expanded), hazard-yellow price-sticker accents, uncoated map-paper cream, map inks (route red, water blue, park green, district tints), manual body in Barlow Condensed with tabular numerals. Everything printed flat with ink outlines; no glow, no glass.

STORY: Visitor sees a 1997 box with the live city through its window, gets the joke and the genre at once, presses the sticker, and is driving within seconds; manual and stickers explain the rest in play.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed box front: logo upper-left at display scale, a die-cut window (about 60% of the viewport) showing the attract-mode city, yellow starburst price sticker as the PLAY action at the window's lower-right corner, a box-edge strip with the box copy. Phone: logo top, window centre, sticker bottom.

FORM: Big-Box Edition, rank 1 of my grounded list (IMPECCABLE’S PICK chosen over the roll). seed:f3b23f0d2a5e1616. Signature interaction: pressing PLAY grows the die-cut window to full screen, so the box opens into the game. Motion grammar: stickers slap on (fast scale-down with a slight rotation, exponential ease-out); banners slam in once; nothing loops for decoration.
