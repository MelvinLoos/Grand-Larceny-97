On a phone, drag anywhere on the left half to walk or drive. The car heads where you point. The buttons on the right jack cars, fire, brake and honk.

The river is deadly: take the bridges, or hit the broken drawbridge ramps flat out for a stunt jump. In a sinking car, press F to bail out and swim for a ladder.

The streets of Neon Bay

  • Gold arrow: go there. Meters count down as you close in. Between jobs the arrow turns cyan and points at the nearest ringing payphone booth; answer it on foot.
  • Red arrow: kill or destroy it. Jobs raise your cash multiplier.
  • Blue arrow: the nearest Spray-B-Gone. Drive into the garage bay for $1,000: full repairs, new paint and five seconds of probation. No cop cars or tanks.
  • Fuse Box Bombs (Harbor, $5,000) fits a car bomb. Press B to arm it.
  • Harbor Export: sell cars by condition. Tick all four on the order board for a $25,000 bonus.
  • Heat badges: one cools off if you lie low. Two or more and they won't stop coming.
