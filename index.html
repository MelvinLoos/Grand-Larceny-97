The streets of Neon Bay

  • Yellow arrow: the nearest ringing payphone. Walk up to answer, and the pager tells you the job.
  • Red arrow: your current target. Jobs raise your cash multiplier.
  • Blue arrow: the nearest Spray-B-Gone. Roll in slowly for free paint, full repairs and zero heat.
  • Heat badges: one cools off if you lie low. Two or more and they won't stop coming.
